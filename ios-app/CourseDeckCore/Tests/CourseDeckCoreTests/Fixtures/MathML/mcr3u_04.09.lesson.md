### Transformations

What does the word transformation mean?

In general, a transformation is a change in shape, appearance, size, or position of a function.

We can refer to the result of a transformation as an _image_ of the original function or feature.

Transformations can be used to create computer generated art, pictures, commercials, and animated demonstrations containing special effects. Special effects are sometimes a result of changing the parameters in the algebraic representation of sine and cosine functions.

The main goal of this activity is to determine the role of the parameters _a_, _k_, _d_, and _c_, and use the parameters to describe the transformations of sinusoidal functions in the form f x \= a f k x \- d + c . We will then graph the transformed function.

### Summary

-   The general form of a transformed function is y \= a f k x \- d + c
-   The general form of a transformed sine function is y \= a   sin ⁡ \[ k x \- d \] + c
-   The general form of a transformed cosine function is y \= a   cos ⁡ \[ k x \- d \] + c
-   Both sine and cosine functions are called sinusoidal functions

**Note**  
The parameters outside the brackets affect the **y**\-coordinates of the sinusoidal function  
(_a_ and _c_ affect the **y** values)  
The parameters inside the brackets affect the **x**\-coordinates of the sinusoidal function  
_k_ and _d_ affect the **x** values)

The maximum values for this wave will be along the line y = 9. Since amplitude can be defined as the distance between the axis, or midline of the wave, and the maximum, add the amplitude to the y \-value in the equation of the axis.

Minimum value = _c_ − _a_

Minimum value = 6 − 3 = 3

The minimum values for this wave will be along the line y = 3. Since amplitude can be defined as the distance between the axis, or midline of the wave, and the minimum, subtract the amplitude from the y \-value in the equation of the axis.
