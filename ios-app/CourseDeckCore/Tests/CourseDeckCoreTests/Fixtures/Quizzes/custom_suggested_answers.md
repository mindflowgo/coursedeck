<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] They cannot own property
- [ ] Owners have unlimited liability
- [ ] They are separate legal entities
- [ ] They cannot issue shares

--

## Answers

01) B - (explanation) Assets are owned items with value.
02) C - (explanation) A corporation is a separate legal entity.

## Short Answer / Case Problems

01) [Application] Explain two risks of a sole proprietorship.

02) [Communication] Explain why a partnership agreement is important.

### Suggested Answers

01) Rubric: Unlimited liability puts the owner's personal assets at risk; the business may lack continuity or capital. Award one mark for each distinct risk.

02) Rubric: A partnership agreement defines roles, profit sharing, decision-making, and dispute resolution. Award one mark for each area addressed.
