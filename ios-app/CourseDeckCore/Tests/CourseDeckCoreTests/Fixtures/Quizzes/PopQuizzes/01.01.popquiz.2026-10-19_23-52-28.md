## Answers
01) C - (explanation) The lesson states the fundamental accounting equation as Assets – Liabilities = Owner’s Equity.
02) A - (explanation) Owner’s equity is calculated as $85,000 – $32,000 = $53,000.
03) B - (explanation) Creditors focus on liquid assets and the business’s ability to pay its debts.
04) D - (explanation) Bookkeeping is the recording stage, while accounting includes recording, controlling, analyzing, and interpreting information.
05) Liabilities - (explanation) Liabilities are the amounts owed to creditors.
06) Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE) - (explanation) ASPE applies to private businesses without public listings; IFRS applies to publicly listed businesses.
