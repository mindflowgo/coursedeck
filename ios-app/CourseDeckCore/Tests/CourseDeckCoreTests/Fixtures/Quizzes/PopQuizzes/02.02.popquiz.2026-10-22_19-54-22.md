# 2.2 Balance Sheet Transactions Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which of the following correctly describes how a liability account responds to debits and credits?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. A debit increases a liability; a credit decreases it.
- [ ] B. A credit increases a liability; a debit decreases it.
- [ ] C. Both a debit and a credit increase a liability.
- [ ] D. A liability can only be increased when the owner's equity account is debited.
--

02) [Thinking] LR Law Firm receives a $400 cheque from a client in payment of their outstanding account. Both Cash and Accounts Receivable are asset accounts. What is the overall effect of this transaction on the accounting equation and total assets?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. Total assets increase by $400 because Cash is debited.
- [ ] B. Total assets decrease by $400 because Accounts Receivable is credited.
- [ ] C. Total assets remain unchanged because one asset increases by $400 while another asset decreases by $400.
- [ ] D. Liabilities decrease by $400 and owner's equity increases by $400.
--

03) [Application] A Cash T-account contains debit entries of $10,000, $5,000, and $400, and a single credit entry of $650. What is the new balance in the account and on which side is it recorded?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. $15,400 on the debit side
- [ ] B. $14,750 on the credit side
- [ ] C. $650 on the debit side
- [ ] D. $14,750 on the debit side
--

04) [Communication] A classmate asks why opening balances for asset accounts are always entered on the debit (left) side of a T-account. Which explanation is the most accurate response?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. Because assets appear on the left-hand side of the balance sheet, and accounts increase on the side on which they appear on the balance sheet.
- [ ] B. Because assets normally carry a credit balance, so they must be placed on the opposite side to correct them.
- [ ] C. Because the double-entry system requires every account to begin on the debit side regardless of type.
- [ ] D. Because the total of asset balances is always larger than the total of liabilities and owner's equity.
--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The fundamental accounting principle requiring that the total dollar amount of debits must equal the total dollar amount of credits in every transaction is known as the _______ system.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The complete group of accounts—all of the asset, liability, and owner's equity accounts together in an accounting system—is referred to as the _______.

--

## Answers
01) B - (explanation) Liabilities appear on the right-hand (credit) side of the balance sheet, so they increase on the credit side and decrease on the debit side.
02) C - (explanation) Cash is debited (increases) $400 and Accounts Receivable is credited (decreases) $400. Since both are assets, the increases and decreases offset, leaving total assets unchanged.
03) D - (explanation) Debit total = 10,000 + 5,000 + 400 = 15,400; credit total = 650. Since 15,400 − 650 = 14,750 and the larger total is on the debit side, the balance is $14,750 debit.
04) A - (explanation) Assets sit on the left side of the balance sheet, and the rule is that accounts increase on the same side on which they appear; therefore asset opening balances are recorded on the debit side.
05) Double-entry - (explanation) The double-entry system is the foundation for recording all transactions, ensuring that debits always equal credits (DR = CR).
06) General ledger (ledger) - (explanation) A ledger is a group of accounts; the general ledger contains all the Assets, Liabilities, and Owner's Equity accounts.
