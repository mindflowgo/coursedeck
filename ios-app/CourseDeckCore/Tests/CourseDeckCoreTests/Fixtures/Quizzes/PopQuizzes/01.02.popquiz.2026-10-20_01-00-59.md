# Types of Business Organizations Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which form of business organization is legally recognized as a separate entity that files its own income tax return and exists independently of its owners?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Sole proprietorship
- [ ] B) General partnership
- [ ] C) Corporation
- [ ] D) Limited partnership

--

02) [Thinking] An investor wants to put money into a business but has no interest in managing day-to-day operations and wants to protect her personal savings. Which arrangement best fits her goals?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Become a general partner so she can share in decision-making
- [ ] B) Become a limited partner, since her liability stops at her registered investment
- [ ] C) Operate as a sole proprietorship to keep all profits
- [ ] D) Become a general partner in a limited partnership to run the business

--

03) [Application] Clara owns a thriving bakery as a sole proprietorship. Her profits are high, she wants to keep full control of the business, and she wants her personal home and savings protected from business debts. Which option best meets all of her goals?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Remain a sole proprietorship and simply buy more insurance
- [ ] B) Form a general partnership with a friend
- [ ] C) Become a public corporation listed on a stock exchange
- [ ] D) Incorporate as a private corporation with limited liability

--

04) [Communication] Canadian law requires corporations to include "Limited" or "Incorporated" in their name. Which statement best explains the purpose of this requirement?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) It signals to the public that the business is a legal entity separate from its owners, whose liability is limited
- [ ] B) It indicates the business is owned and operated by only one person
- [ ] C) It shows that the business pays no income tax on its net income
- [ ] D) It confirms that the business is a partnership with at least one limited partner

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Because a sole proprietorship is not a separate legal entity, the owner reports the business's net income on their _______ income tax return.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] In a _______ partnership, every partner has unlimited personal liability for the debts of the business.

--

## Answers
01) C - (explanation) A corporation is a separate legal entity that can sue and be sued, has an unlimited life, and pays tax on its income to federal and provincial governments; proprietorships and partnerships are not separate legal entities and are taxed through the owners' personal returns.
02) B - (explanation) Limited partners contribute capital but have no liability beyond their registered investment in the partnership, and they generally stay out of operations; only the general partner(s) assume legal responsibility for the partnership's debts.
03) D - (explanation) A private corporation gives Clara limited liability (her personal assets are protected), is restricted to 50 shareholders, and cannot raise funds on public stock markets, so she keeps control and ownership — unlike a public corporation or a partnership.
04) A - (explanation) The "Ltd." or "Inc." designation publicly identifies the business as a separate legal entity, alerting customers and creditors that the owners' liability is limited to their investment in the company.
05) personal - (explanation) A proprietorship does not pay its own taxes; the owner adds business net income to other income and pays personal income tax on the total, which can be a tax advantage when income is low.
06) general - (explanation) In a general partnership, all partners have unlimited personal liability; a limited partnership addresses this by having limited partners whose liability is capped at their registered investment.
