# 2.1 The Balance Sheet Pop Quiz

## Questions

01) [Knowledge] A business lists the following items on its balance sheet. Which one belongs on the **liabilities** side of the statement?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Accounts Receivable
- [ ] Office Supplies
- [ ] Equipment
- [ ] Unpaid Wages

--

02) [Thinking] A company's balance sheet lists Cash, Accounts Receivable, Office Supplies, and Equipment. Which sequence correctly orders these assets from **most liquid to least liquid**?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Equipment, Accounts Receivable, Cash, Office Supplies
- [ ] Cash, Accounts Receivable, Office Supplies, Equipment
- [ ] Accounts Receivable, Cash, Equipment, Office Supplies
- [ ] Cash, Office Supplies, Equipment, Accounts Receivable

--

03) [Application] A small catering company reports the following: Cash $4,000; Food Supplies $1,500; Equipment $18,000; Land and Buildings $120,000; Bank Loan $6,000; Unpaid Wages $2,300; Accounts Payable $4,200; Mortgage on the Building $70,000. Using the balance sheet equation, what is the owner's equity?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] $61,000
- [ ] $82,500
- [ ] $143,500
- [ ] $65,200

--

04) [Communication] A business owns a building valued at $500,000 and still owes $230,000 on its mortgage. Which presentation best follows the rules for preparing a balance sheet?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Show "Building, net" at $270,000 under assets and leave the mortgage off the statement
- [ ] Show Building $500,000 under assets and record the $230,000 owed as part of owner's equity
- [ ] Show Building $500,000 under assets and Mortgage Payable $230,000 under liabilities
- [ ] Show only the $270,000 net amount as the Total Assets figure

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Assets on a balance sheet are listed in order of _______, meaning the ease and speed with which they could be converted into cash.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Liabilities are ordered according to the _______ rule, which lists debts that come due soonest ahead of longer-term debt such as a mortgage.

--

## Answers
01) D - Unpaid Wages is money the business owes to its employees, so it is a liability. Accounts Receivable, Office Supplies, and Equipment are all items owned (assets).
02) B - Liquidity runs from cash (already money) through receivables and supplies to long-lived equipment. Equipment is listed last because it has the longest expected life before being converted to cash.
03) A - Total assets = $4,000 + $1,500 + $18,000 + $120,000 = $143,500. Total liabilities = $6,000 + $2,300 + $4,200 + $70,000 = $82,500. Owner's equity = Assets − Liabilities = $143,500 − $82,500 = $61,000.
04) C - All financial details must be fully disclosed: the building is shown at its full $500,000 value as an asset, and the $230,000 still owed is shown separately as Mortgage Payable. Netting the two would hide information from users of the statement.
05) Liquidity - The order of assets reflects how quickly each can be turned into cash, with cash always listed first.
06) Maturity date - Debts due soonest (e.g., Accounts Payable in about 30 days) appear before medium-term debt (a one-year bank loan), which appears before long-term debt (a 30-year mortgage).
