# What Skills Will I Need? Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which option correctly identifies the three employability skill areas used by the Conference Board of Canada?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Reading, numeracy, writing
- [ ] B) Technical, financial, entrepreneurial
- [ ] C) Fundamental, personal management, teamwork
- [ ] D) Communication, collaboration, creativity

--

02) [Thinking] A student says, “Essential skills are only the basic literacy skills you need to get your first job.” Which response best corrects this misconception?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Essential skills are only reading and writing; all other skills are job-specific.
- [ ] B) Essential skills are nine broad skills, such as digital, collaboration, adaptability, and creativity, that support work, learning, and life across many occupations.
- [ ] C) Essential skills are identical to the Conference Board’s three employability skill areas.
- [ ] D) Essential skills are assessed only by employers and cannot be self-assessed.

--

03) [Application] Priya wants to improve a skill she can use both at work and in daily life. She practises working with classmates on a group project, resolving disagreements, and sharing tasks. Which essential skill is she mainly developing?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Collaboration
- [ ] B) Numeracy
- [ ] C) Writing
- [ ] D) Digital

--

04) [Communication] Which sentence best explains why self-assessment of skills is useful when choosing a career?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) It guarantees you will be hired for the job you want.
- [ ] B) It replaces the need for employer feedback or peer assessment.
- [ ] C) It proves essential skills cannot change over time.
- [ ] D) It helps you identify strengths and gaps so you can choose suitable career paths and plan improvement.

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The Government of Canada identifies nine _______ skills—including reading, numeracy, digital, collaboration, and adaptability—that provide a foundation for work, learning, and life.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The Conference Board of Canada groups employability skills into fundamental skills, personal management skills, and _______ skills.

--

## Answers
01) C - Fundamental, personal management, and teamwork skills are the three major areas identified by the Conference Board of Canada.
02) B - Essential skills are broader than basic literacy and include nine skills used across occupations and daily life.
03) A - Working with others, resolving disagreements, and sharing tasks are key parts of collaboration.
04) D - Self-assessment helps people recognize their strengths and areas for growth so they can make informed career choices and plan improvements.
05) essential - The nine essential skills are foundational skills for work, learning, and life.
06) teamwork - Teamwork skills are the third employability skill area, alongside fundamental and personal management skills.
