# Creating Pathways to Success Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Ontario's education and career/life planning framework (Creating Pathways to Success) is built on four guiding questions. Which of the following is **NOT** one of those four questions?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Who am I?
- [ ] What are my opportunities?
- [ ] How much money will I earn in my chosen career?
- [ ] What is my plan for achieving my goals?

--

02) [Thinking] The lesson stresses that the education and career/life planning process is **cyclical**, not linear. Which statement best explains why?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Because students should only answer each of the four questions once, in Unit 1
- [ ] Because as students gain deeper self-knowledge and encounter new opportunities, they are expected to revisit the earlier questions with fresh insight
- [ ] Because the four questions must be answered in a single sitting before moving on
- [ ] Because the questions only apply to adults who are already employed

--

03) [Application] Maya has just finished the assignments in Unit 2 and wants to know when her portfolio will actually be graded. According to the lesson, when does portfolio assessment occur?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] After every single notebook task is completed
- [ ] Only once, at the end of Unit 1
- [ ] Only at the very end of the course, after the culminating project
- [ ] Twice—once at the end of Unit 2 and again at the end of Unit 4

--

04) [Communication] Before adding a notebook response to your Unit 1 portfolio, the lesson advises that you should do which of the following?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Submit your rough draft exactly as you first wrote it
- [ ] Review, revise, and edit it into a polished one-page document in a format of your choice
- [ ] Delete it entirely and start over from scratch in a different format
- [ ] Keep it only in a paper notebook and never digitize it

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Ontario's education and career/life planning framework is described as a four-step _______ process built on four guiding questions.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] In the flow of the course, Unit 1 navigates _______ as you focus on personal knowledge and management skills, while Unit 2 shifts to interpersonal knowledge and skills.

--

## Answers
01) C - (explanation) The four framework questions are "Who am I?", "What are my opportunities?", "Who do I want to become?", and "What is my plan for achieving my goals?"—salary is not one of the four guiding questions.
02) B - (explanation) The process is ongoing and cyclical; students regularly return to the four questions with greater knowledge of themselves and their opportunities, refining their answers as they grow.
03) D - (explanation) The lesson states portfolios are submitted twice for grading—once at the end of Unit 2 and again at the end of Unit 4.
04) B - (explanation) The lesson directs students to review, revise, and edit notebook answers into a polished one-page document (e.g., Word or PDF), adding images or links as appropriate, before adding them to the portfolio.
05) Inquiry - (explanation) The CPS framework is a four-step inquiry process built on the four linked questions.
06) Inward - (explanation) Unit 1 navigates inward (personal knowledge and skills); Unit 2 navigates outward (interpersonal knowledge and skills); Units 3 and 4 navigate toward the future.
