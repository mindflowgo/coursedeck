# 2.4 The Income Statement Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which of the following accounts would be classified as a revenue account and reported on the income statement?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Equipment Repairs
- [ ] Income from Concessions
- [ ] Accounts Receivable
- [ ] Bank Loan

--

02) [Thinking] A business earned $48,000 in revenue and incurred $52,500 in expenses during the same accounting period. How should this result be presented on the income statement?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] $4,500 Net Income in the right-hand column with double lines beneath it
- [ ] $96,500 shown as Total Revenue
- [ ] $4,500 recorded as a liability on the balance sheet
- [ ] $4,500 Net Loss shown in brackets with double lines beneath it

--

03) [Application] A law firm completes legal work for a client and issues an invoice for $1,500, but the client has not yet paid. Under the accrual basis of accounting, how should this transaction be recorded?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] Debit Accounts Receivable $1,500; Credit Revenue $1,500
- [ ] Debit Cash $1,500; Credit Revenue $1,500
- [ ] Debit Revenue $1,500; Credit Accounts Receivable $1,500
- [ ] No entry is recorded until the client pays the invoice

--

04) [Communication] A creditor is reviewing a company's income statement to judge whether the business can repay a loan. Why does the matching principle matter to that reader?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] It guarantees that the business will always report a profit each period
- [ ] It removes the need for the business to define an accounting period
- [ ] It ensures expenses are recorded in the same period as the revenue they helped generate, so net income is not overstated or misleading
- [ ] It allows the owner's personal withdrawals to be reported as operating expenses

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The financial statement that presents the revenue, expenses, and net income or loss for a specific period of time is called the _______.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The accounting principle requiring that expenses be recorded and matched with the revenue they helped to generate in the same accounting period is called the _______.

--

## Answers
01) B - (explanation) Income from Concessions is money earned from sales, so it is a revenue account. Equipment Repairs is an expense, Accounts Receivable is an asset, and Bank Loan is a liability (all on the balance sheet except the expense).
02) D - (explanation) When expenses exceed revenue, the difference is a net loss; accounting convention shows a loss in brackets with double lines under the final figure.
03) A - (explanation) Under accrual accounting, revenue is recorded when it is earned (invoiced), even if cash has not been received. This creates a debit to Accounts Receivable (asset increases) and a credit to Revenue (revenue increases owner's equity).
04) C - (explanation) The matching principle links expenses to the revenue they generated in the same period, preventing costs from being shifted to another period and distorting net income — critical information for a creditor assessing repayment ability.
05) income statement - (explanation) The income statement reports revenue, expenses, and the resulting net income or net loss over an accounting period, unlike the balance sheet, which shows financial position on a specific date.
06) matching principle - (explanation) The matching principle underpins accrual accounting by requiring expenses to be recorded in the same period as the revenue they helped earn, so the reported net income is accurate.
