# 1.2 Knowing Yourself Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which field of study uses assessments and questionnaires to measure psychological traits such as personality, interests, values, and aptitudes?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Social identity theory
- [ ] B) Psychometrics
- [ ] C) Employability skills
- [ ] D) Self-actualization

--

02) [Thinking] A student scores highest in Holland’s Conventional type and lowest in Artistic. Which work environment is likely the poorest fit for this student?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) A structured accounting firm with clear procedures
- [ ] B) A records-management office using ordered systems
- [ ] C) A data-entry role with regular schedules
- [ ] D) A freeform design studio with minimal rules

--

03) [Application] Maya values environmental conservation and has strong science and communication skills. She wants a career that aligns with her values and aptitudes. Which action best applies self-knowledge to her career planning?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Pursuing a role in environmental education or sustainable policy that uses science and communication
- [ ] B) Choosing the highest-paying job regardless of environmental impact
- [ ] C) Avoiding science because values matter more than skills
- [ ] D) Taking a job that ignores her values to test adaptability

--

04) [Communication] In a workplace team meeting, a co-worker says, “My MBTI result says I’m an introvert, so I can’t lead or present.” What is the most accurate response based on the lesson?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) “You’re right—MBTI letters measure your skill level, so avoid leadership.”
- [ ] B) “MBTI types never change, so your role is fixed.”
- [ ] C) “The MBTI indicates preferences, not ability; you can still develop leadership and presentation skills.”
- [ ] D) “Introverts should only work alone and never communicate with teams.”

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The assessment that identifies four personality preferences and 16 possible personality types is called the Myers-Briggs Type _______.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] A person’s overall perception of who they are, including their attributes, values, interests, and beliefs, is known as their _______.

--

## Answers
01) B - Psychometrics is the research area or science of measuring psychological traits through assessments and questionnaires.
02) D - Conventional personalities prefer order and structure, while Artistic personalities prefer creativity and flexibility; a freeform design studio is the poorest fit for high Conventional/low Artistic.
03) A - Applying self-knowledge means aligning career choices with both values and aptitudes; environmental education or sustainable policy fits Maya’s conservation values and science/communication skills.
04) C - The MBTI describes preferences, not skill levels or fixed abilities, so introversion does not prevent someone from developing leadership or presentation skills.
05) Indicator - The full name is the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator, which identifies personality preferences and 16 types.
06) self-concept - Self-concept is how a person perceives or thinks about themselves, including attributes, values, interests, and beliefs.
