# 1.1 Introduction to Accounting Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] Which term describes the owner's remaining claim on a business's assets once all creditor claims have been settled?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Liquidity
- [ ] B) Total assets
- [ ] C) Owner's equity
- [ ] D) Book value of liabilities

--

02) [Thinking] A company reports total liabilities of $85,000 and owner's equity of $40,000. Based on the fundamental accounting equation, what must its total assets be?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) $125,000
- [ ] B) $45,000
- [ ] C) $85,000
- [ ] D) $40,000

--

03) [Application] Clara's new business has cash of $6,000, equipment valued at $14,000, an outstanding bank loan of $9,000, and $2,000 owed to a supplier. What is her owner's equity?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) $20,000
- [ ] B) $11,000
- [ ] C) $23,000
- [ ] D) $9,000

--

04) [Communication] Which statement best explains the relationship between accounting and bookkeeping?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A) Bookkeeping involves analyzing and interpreting records, while accounting is limited to recording daily transactions.
- [ ] B) Bookkeeping is the recording stage of the process, while accounting also includes controlling, analyzing, and interpreting that information for decision making.
- [ ] C) Accounting and bookkeeping are interchangeable terms for the same task performed only by external auditors.
- [ ] D) Accounting is performed only by government agencies, while bookkeeping is done solely by business owners.

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] The relationship Assets − Liabilities = Owner's Equity is known as the _______, and it applies to every type of organization regardless of its size or purpose.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Since 2011, Canadian accountants follow IFRS for publicly traded companies and _______ for private businesses that do not have shares traded on a public stock exchange.

--

## Answers
01) C - (explanation) Owner's equity (also called net worth or capital) is the owner's entitlement to the assets after all creditor claims are paid; it is calculated as assets minus liabilities.
02) A - (explanation) Rearranging the fundamental accounting equation gives Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity, so $85,000 + $40,000 = $125,000.
03) D - (explanation) Total assets = $6,000 + $14,000 = $20,000; total liabilities = $9,000 + $2,000 = $11,000; equity = $20,000 − $11,000 = $9,000.
04) B - (explanation) Bookkeeping is only the first part of the accounting process (recording in journals and ledgers); accounting is the broader function that also controls, analyzes, and interprets that information for decision making.
05) Fundamental accounting equation - (explanation) It is also called the balance sheet equation and is the basis for determining an entity's financial position.
06) ASPE (Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises) - (explanation) The AcSB sets both IFRS, for publicly listed companies, and ASPE, for private enterprises without public share listings.
