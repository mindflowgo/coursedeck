# 1.4 The Accounting Profession Pop Quiz

## Questions
01) [Knowledge] In Canada today, what is the unified professional accounting designation that replaced the former CA, CGA, and CMA designations?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. Chartered Accountant (CA)
- [ ] B. Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA)
- [ ] C. Certified Management Accountant (CMA)
- [ ] D. Certified General Accountant (CGA)

--

02) [Thinking] A company wants an accountant who is not an employee of the company to review its financial records and provide assurance to outside investors. Which type of audit is being described?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. An internal audit, because the auditor works for the company
- [ ] B. A tax audit, because Revenue Canada always requests it
- [ ] C. A payroll audit, because it checks employee wages
- [ ] D. An independent audit, because an outside auditor conducts the review

--

03) [Application] Maya enjoys negotiating budgets, explaining financial information to different departments, and using spreadsheets to combine data from several sources. Which accounting role and skill emphasis best fit her strengths?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. Budget analyst, using interpersonal communication and analytical/computer skills
- [ ] B. Audit clerk, using only basic record-checking skills
- [ ] C. Forensic accountant, focusing mainly on criminal investigations
- [ ] D. Payroll clerk, focusing mainly on entering hours and issuing paycheques

--

04) [Communication] Why is it inaccurate to say that every job in accounting requires a CPA designation?
<Multiple-Choice>
- [ ] A. Because CPA stands for Canadian Payroll Association
- [ ] B. Because all accounting jobs are unregulated and require no training
- [ ] C. Because some roles, such as payroll clerk or audit clerk, may require high-school or college training and basic accounting skills rather than full professional certification
- [ ] D. Because auditors are not considered accountants

--

05) [Fill-in-the-Blank] Accountants employed by agencies such as Revenue Canada or provincial sales-tax departments are known as _______ accountants.

--

06) [Fill-in-the-Blank] When an audit is performed by a member of the organization’s own staff, it is called a(n) _______ audit.

--

## Answers
01) B - Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) is the unified Canadian designation created by merging the former CA, CGA, and CMA groups.
02) D - An independent audit is conducted by an outside auditor, which helps reassure investors and lenders about the accuracy of financial records.
03) A - Budget analysts need interpersonal and negotiation skills, and they often use analytical and computer skills to develop and manage financial plans.
04) C - Not all accounting jobs require a CPA; roles such as audit clerk or payroll clerk may require less formal certification or education.
05) government - Government accountants work for public agencies such as Revenue Canada or provincial sales-tax departments.
06) internal - An internal audit is conducted by someone who is a member of the organization’s staff.
