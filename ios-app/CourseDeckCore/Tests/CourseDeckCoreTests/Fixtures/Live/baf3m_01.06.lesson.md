# Think like an accountant!

## Assessment for Feedback and Grade

This is your first assessment for feedback and a grade. This assessment is worth 15% of your course grade.

The teacher will assess your work using the following rubric. Before submitting your assessment, review the rubric to ensure that you are meeting the success criteria to the best of your ability.

When you are ready, submit your assessment for feedback and a grade, by selecting the “Assignment” link and following the submission directions.

![Mortarboard and diploma ready for business|50%](./assets/img/baf3m_01.06.01.jpg)

**Let's get started!**

Are you ready to be an accountant? Sure, you don’t have the technical skills just yet, but do you have the mindset?

Imagine that you have graduated from college or university with an accounting dilpoma/certificate/degree, have worked in an accounting related field for several years, and you now decide to open up your own accounting firm, catering to small businesses in the community.

Based on what you learned about the accounting profession and designations in Unit 1, complete the following task.

### Instructions:

Create a slide presentation introducing yourself and your new accounting firm.

You will also need to write a script of what you would say as each slide appears on the screen. Use template [1.6 Think like an accountant! Script template](./assets/locker_docs/baf3m_01.06.01.pdf). Alternatively, you may wish to create your own using a program of your choice. Write in full sentence. You must submit your script along with your slide presentation.

It is also important to number your slides, as this will help you in organizing your script.

If you are using PowerPoint:

-   Select **Insert** tab of the ribbon
-   Click on **Insert Slide** number icon
-   Click on **Apply to All**

If you are using Google Slides:

-   Select **Insert**
-   Click on **Slide Numbers**
-   Select **On** and **Apply**

**Important:** the **Help** feature in any software that you use can provide you with Step by Step instructions on how to do something. **Youtube** can also be helpful by providing tutorials that you can view.

  

Refer back to your Notebook, assignment and the content in this Unit. You are also encouraged to do some research, but remember to reference your sources in APA style.

Your presentation should include the following:

-   **Title slide** - include your name and the name of your company
-   **All about your slides** -
    -   Your educational background - Name of college/university. Name of program and diploma/degree, Details about your program (i.e. length of program, courses of study or area of focus, co-op opportunities etc…)
    -   Designation or certification you acquired- What is it? Why it important? What did you need to do to obtain it?
    -   Your professional experience in accounting related jobs- Where did you work? What was your job title, job description, duties and skills acquired?
    -   Your skills and talents as they relate to transferable skills and the accounting profession- (refer to the Transferable skills you learned about in Learning Activity 1.4) What are your skills and talents? How will they be helpful to you and your company? Why are skills and qualifications important? Perhaps you may also want to address who works with you and what qualifications and skills they have?
-   **All about your firm slides** -
    -   Type of business ownership your company will be- Advantages and disadvantages of your chosen type. Why have you selected it over the other types?
    -   Technology in your firm- How will you use technology in performing accounting tasks? Why is it important? What are the advantages and disadvantages of using technology? What technology tools will you use? How will it impact accounting functions?
    -   Implementation of ethical accounting practices- How does your firm embrace ethical accounting practices? Why are ethical accounting practices important to your firm? What role does it play in your firm? How will you use and administer it?
-   **Why do business with an accounting firm slides** -
    -   Why would it be beneficial for a business in your community to hire an accounting firm? How can your services help small business owners improve their decision making? Perhaps you can explain to them the purpose of accounting and highlight the users and uses of accounting.
-   **Conclusion slide**
-   **APA references slide**

Before you submit your presentation to your teacher, ensure you have completed the following:

### Content

-   Does my presentation demonstrate my knowledge about the accounting profession?
-   Does my presentation demonstrate my knowledge of the types of business ownership?
-   Does my presentation demonstrate my knowledge about the educational background of an accountant?
-   Does my presentation examine technology and its impact on how to perform accounting functions?
-   Does my presentation demonstrate my understanding of the role of ethics in accounting?
-   Does my presentation demonstrate my ability to use research skills and apply real life scenarios to my company and professional profile?

### Design and Layout (they are an important part of your presentation)

-   Is my presentation free of spelling and grammatical errors?
-   Are the images and videos used relevant to the topic, text, and references?
-   Is there enough white space among text, images, and videos?
-   Is the font consistent, appropriate, and readable with no more than two or three sizes throughout?
-   Is the colour of background and text consistent throughout?
-   Have I limited the words on each slide?
