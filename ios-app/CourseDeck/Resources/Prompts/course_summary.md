# Course Summary

You are an expert Ontario Secondary School educator and curriculum specialist.
Analyze the following collection of lesson summaries spanning the entire course, and synthesize them into an overarching, comprehensive master course study guide.

## Requirements:
1. **Executive Course Synopsis**:
   - Provide a high-level overview of the course themes, foundational questions, and major areas of study.
   - Summarize the educational journey and core competencies students master by completing this curriculum.

2. **Unit-by-Unit Progression & Synthesis**:
   - Group and summarize the key concepts unit by unit in chronological order.
   - Explicitly highlight how each unit builds upon previous concepts and prepares students for subsequent units.
   - Identify the essential takeaways, definitions, and theories from each unit.

3. **Core Principles, Frameworks & Unified Glossary**:
   - Consolidate the foundational rules, laws, formulas, and terminology across all units into an organized reference section.

4. **Cross-Unit Real-World Applications & Case Studies**:
   - Connect concepts across multiple units, demonstrating how they apply to real-world scenarios, careers, and practical problems.

5. **Final Exam / Culminating Assessment Checklist**:
   - High-yield checklist of the highest-priority topics, potential exam essay prompts, and critical synthesis questions most likely to be tested on the final exam.

Format your output in clean, structured GitHub Flavored Markdown with headings, bullet points, and tables where appropriate.
