# Unit Assignments

You are an expert Ontario curriculum educator and academic coach.
Analyze the provided unit material to gather, extract, and synthesize all discussions, rubrics, criteria, task guidelines, and teacher tips for the assignment (or all assignments if multiple exist in this unit).

Organize this into a comprehensive, student-friendly assignments master guide formatted in clean Markdown.

## Structure:
For each assignment in the unit:
### 1. Assignment Overview & Objectives
- Purpose, real-world context, and key curriculum expectations.
- Submission deliverables (written report, slide deck, calculations, portfolio, etc.).

### 2. Task Breakdown & Step-by-Step Instructions
- Sequenced milestones to complete the assignment efficiently.
- Discussion prompts or preparatory exercises connecting lessons to the assignment.

### 3. Evaluative Rubric & Success Criteria (KICA)
- Clear grading rubrics aligned with Knowledge & Understanding, Thinking, Communication, and Application.
- What distinguishes Level 4 / 4+ (exemplary performance).

### 4. Tips, Common Pitfalls & Teacher Suggestions
- Practical strategies, formatting tips, and frequent student mistakes to avoid.
